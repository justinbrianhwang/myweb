About

I am an independent researcher working on artificial intelligence and quantum computing. I studied software and physics at Yonsei University, where I was an undergraduate researcher at the RAISE Lab.

Research direction. Making AI systems robust and reliable, from quantum-enhanced machine learning to safety-critical applications in autonomous driving and semiconductor design.

Questions I care about. How do we build AI that doesn't break under uncertainty? Where can quantum computing do what classical methods can't?

Next. Graduate research at the intersection of quantum computing and trustworthy AI, connecting theory with real-world deployment.
